EPL Pricing Intelligence: Definitive Technical Specification & Data Science Handbook | Version 2.0

1. SYSTEM INTENT & VISION

The EPL Pricing Intelligence System is a state-of-the-art decision layer designed to bridge the gap between Sports Sentiment and Revenue Yield Management. While legacy systems rely on "Flat Tiering" (e.g., Category A/B/C), this system implements a High-Fidelity Parametric Model.

Core Philosophy

We treat every seat not as a fixed asset, but as a dynamic financial instrument whose value is driven by the Stakes of the match, the Scarcity of the zone, and the Willingness-to-Pay (WTP) of specific fan segments derived via Bayesian modeling.

2. DOMAIN PRIMER: THE EPL ENVIRONMENT

To validate the system's logic, one must understand the three commercial "Pressure Points" of the Premier League:

2.1 The Points Paradox

In the EPL, a match between the 1st and 2nd place teams in August is worth 3 points. The same match in May is worth 3 points, but its Commercial Value is exponentially higher because of the Title Race proximity. Our system captures this via the Urgency Multiplier.

2.2 The Regulatory Anchor

Rule L.19 (Away Allocation): Mandatory 10% capacity or 3,000 seats. Rule L.20 (Price Cap): Maximum £30 for Away fans. The system locks these seats as a constant, ensuring revenue from these zones is tracked but never "optimized" at the risk of league sanctions.

3.1 Production Data Acquisition Strategy

A. The Ticketing Webhook (Sales Velocity)

Unlike the POC's synthetic curves, production velocity is derived from Live Sales Events. Every ticket sold fires a webhook from AXS/Ticketmaster to our ingestion endpoint. The system compares the cumulative total against the DTW Archetype Medoid to detect demand spikes early.

B. The Standings Sync (Stakes Calculation)

Immediately following the final whistle of any Premier League match, a push notification from Opta triggers a re-calculation of the League Table. This updates the match_stakes_score for all active matches involving affected clubs.

C. The Lineup Pulse (Star Power)

Exactly 60 minutes before kickoff, official team sheets are released. Our system parses these squads via the Genius Sports API, updates the star_power_index, and runs a final LP Re-optimization for any remaining general sale or hospitality inventory.

4. THE STAKES ENGINE: CALCULUS

The StakesEngine (stakes_engine.py) is a stateless calculator that translates standings into emotion.

4.1 The Urgency Equation

Linear Decay Formula U(MW) = 0.1 + (MW - 1) * (0.9 / 37)

Where MW is the Matchweek. This ensures that a relegation battle in MW 38 has 10x the urgency of MW 1.

4.2 Multi-Narrative Fusion

The engine computes four independent "Contention Scores":

  • Title Score: Closeness to 1st place points.
  • Top 4 Score: Closeness to Champions League qualification boundary.
  • Relegation Score: Proximity to the 18th place "Drop Zone."
  • European Score: Contention for secondary UEFA spots (5th-7th).

Final Score = MAX(Title, Top4, Relegation, Euro). This ensures the system always prices based on the most compelling storyline for a given match.

5. CONJOINT ENGINE: HB-MNL MODELING

Fan preferences are not guessed; they are derived using **Hierarchical Bayesian Multinomial Logit** (HB-MNL).

5.1 The Utility Function

Stochastic Choice Model U_{ij} = \beta_{i, price} \times P_j + \sum \beta_{i, k} \times A_{kj} + \epsilon_{ij}

5.2 Bayesian Priors (PyMC Specification)

The DS team must ensure the beta_price remains strictly negative to reflect the law of demand:

mu_price = Normal("mu_price", -3.0, 1.0)
beta_price = -exp(mu_price + price_offset * sigma)

By using the negative exponential of a normal distribution, we ensure the optimizer never suggests a positive price-attendance correlation.

6. INVENTORY ARCHITECTURE

The InventoryManager (inventory_manager.py) segments stadium capacity into three dynamic price-sensitive zones:

Zone 1: Dynamic Lower (The Atmosphere Anchor)

55% of Dynamic Inventory. Highly inelastic due to pitch proximity. Priced for core fans.

Zone 2: Dynamic Upper (The Revenue Lever)

35% of Dynamic Inventory. Balanced elasticity. Primary zone for yield harvesting.

Zone 3: Dynamic General (The Value Buffer)

10% of Dynamic Inventory. Highly elastic. Used to fill the stadium for low-stakes matches.

7. FORECASTING: STACKED ENSEMBLE

The forecasting engine implements a 4-Layer stack to predict overall_fill_rate.

7.1 Layer 1: Temporal Decomposition

  • STL: Period=19. Extracts the "Club Momentum" signal.
  • SARIMA: Period=38. Models league-wide seasonality (e.g., the "August Lull" or "May Surge").

8. LP OPTIMIZER: MILP SOLVER

The EPLLPOptimizer (lp_optimizer_epl.py) is the system's "Brain." It uses Mixed-Integer Linear Programming.

8.1 The Elasticity Matrix

Each zone has a distinct Price Elasticity of Demand (ε):

  • Hospitality: ε = -0.10 (Virtually Inelastic)
  • Lower Tier: ε = -0.55
  • Upper Tier: ε = -0.75
  • General Sale: ε = -1.10 (Highly Elastic)

8.2 Monotonicity: The Brand Protection Rule

The solver enforces Strict Monotonicity: Hospitality > Lower > Upper > General. This ensures that a seat in the "Upper Tier" is never more expensive than "Hospitality," protecting the club's premium branding architecture.

9. LIVE SIGNAL PIPELINE

The system is "Alive"—it recalibrates based on real-time data ingestion.

Velocity Surge Logic

If Velocity_T14 > 1.25 (Actual sales 25% higher than Archetype Medoid), the forecasting engine scales the overall_fill_rate by 1.1x, triggering the LP Optimizer to push prices toward the Conjoint-derived Ceiling.
