## 2. Technical Justifications
1. **ST Cap**: Clubs are capping season tickets to facilitate "Paid Membership" ballot systems. This creates a higher-yield "match-by-match" market.
2. **Hospitality Delta**: The Big Six average 10-15% hospitality. Mid-table clubs average 5-8%. This delta is critical for PSR compliance.
3. **Away Constant**: The Premier League Handbook Section L mandates 3,000 seats. This is a non-negotiable constraint for the LP Optimizer.
